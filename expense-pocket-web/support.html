Support

Back up your data

Go to Settings and select Export backup. Store the JSON file somewhere secure. To restore it, choose Restore backup and select the file. Restoring replaces your current entries.

Delete your data

Choose Delete all data in Settings. You will also need to delete any backup files you exported.

Bill reminders

Due dates are displayed inside the app. This version does not send background notifications. Please use your calendar or another reminder app for alerts.
