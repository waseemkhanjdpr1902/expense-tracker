Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Information stored on your device

Expense Pocket lets you enter transactions, descriptions, categories, budgets and bill dates. This version stores that information in your browser's local storage on your device. It does not send those entries to a Expense Pocket server. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to see them.

Backups and deletion

You can export a JSON backup or CSV file to your device, and import a JSON backup. You decide where to store or share exported files. Use Settings → Delete all data to remove entries saved by the app in the current browser. Clearing browser storage may also erase your data. Backups and copies you made must be deleted separately.

Website hosting and fonts

Our hosting provider may receive normal connection information such as IP address, browser information and request logs when you access this website. This site requests fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive connection information. Refer to those providers' policies for their handling of this information.

Advertising, payments and analytics

This version does not integrate advertising, subscriptions, payment processing or analytics. If these are added, this policy will be updated before activation to describe the relevant data practices and choices.

Children

Expense Pocket is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our practices change. Please see the support page for contact information.
